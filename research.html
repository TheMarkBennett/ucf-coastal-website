Facilities & field research

Research across labs and landscapes

UCF Coastal researchers have access to an exceptional range of laboratories, field stations, analytical facilities, computational resources and coastal research sites across UCF and Central Florida. Together, these facilities allow us to study the coast at every scale, from the chemistry and genetics of individual organisms to changing ecosystems, coastal hazards, infrastructure and entire communities.

Our coastal ecology facilities provide a particularly strong connection between the university and Florida's coastline.

Coastal ecology · Field research

Coastal and Estuarine Ecology Laboratory (CEELAB)

Professor Linda Walters’ CEELAB combines UCF campus facilities with Fellers House Field Station in Canaveral National Seashore, providing access to the Indian River Lagoon and Mosquito Lagoon. Research includes oysters, seagrass, mangroves, living shorelines and ecosystem restoration.

Explore Healthy Coast →

Aquatic biogeochemistry · Soil and sediment

Laboratories for water, soil and sediment analysis

Professor Lisa Chambers’ Aquatic Biogeochemistry Laboratory and the Soil and Sediment Laboratory support analysis of water, soils, sediments, nutrients, carbon and greenhouse gases. Dedicated wet labs and instrumentation support water-quality, carbon and nitrogen measurements, gas chromatography and other biogeochemical analyses; researchers also have access to field space, greenhouses, genomics facilities and a research boat.

Marine Genomics Laboratory
