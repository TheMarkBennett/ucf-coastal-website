Research project · Energy resilience

Powering resilient communities with clean energy

Clean, Affordable, and Resilient Energy Systems (CARES) for Socially Vulnerable and At-Risk Communities.

The research question

How can clean energy help when extreme weather strikes?

The CARES project develops a geospatial framework to identify where solar energy and battery storage could have the greatest impact across Central Florida and the Florida Panhandle.

The research examines how hazards such as hurricanes and severe storms can lead to power outages, and how vulnerability varies across communities. It brings together expertise in renewable energy, extreme weather, geospatial science, engineering and social vulnerability.

The framework is intended to help identify locations where solar and solar-plus-storage systems may support access to reliable, affordable power during outages.

Research theme

This project relates to hazard resilience, infrastructure and community preparedness.
