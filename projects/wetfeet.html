Research project · Coastal wetlands

Understanding the future of coastal wetlands

The interplay of nitrogen loading and ecosystem sustainability in threatened wetlands: an extension of the WETFEET project.

The research question

What happens when environmental pressures act together?

WETFEET brings together scientists, land managers and environmental educators to study interacting pressures in coastal wetlands.

At the Guana Tolomato Matanzas National Estuarine Research Reserve in Northeast Florida, the project examines the combined effects of sea-level rise, warming and increased nitrogen on mangroves and salt marshes.

Understanding these changes can help researchers and coastal managers anticipate ecosystem responses. Wetlands provide habitat, store carbon, improve water quality and can help reduce risks to coastal communities.

Research theme

WETFEET connects wetland ecology, environmental change and ecosystem sustainability.
