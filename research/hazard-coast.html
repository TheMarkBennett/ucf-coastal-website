Hazard Coast

Facing hazards, resilient waterfronts. Research helps communities, infrastructure and ecosystems prepare for coastal hazards and adapt to change.

Why this matters

Understand risk. Strengthen resilience.

Sea-level rise, storms, flooding, erosion, marine heat waves and harmful algal blooms can affect coastal communities, ecosystems and infrastructure.

UCF Coastal research described in the faculty content brings together science, engineering, social science and policy to understand hazards, anticipate impacts and develop approaches that support resilience.

Research questions include how coastal hazards interact, which places and populations face greater exposure, and how preparedness, response and recovery can be strengthened.
