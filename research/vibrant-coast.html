Vibrant Coast

Thriving sustainable communities. Explore how coastal economies and communities can flourish while sustaining the natural systems they depend on.

People, economy & place

Coastal wellbeing is connected.

Coastal communities depend on their coastlines for economies, livelihoods, culture and quality of life.

The Vibrant Coast theme brings together social, economic and environmental perspectives on issues such as tourism, fisheries, development, infrastructure and urban planning. It considers how growing populations, industry and changing environmental conditions intersect.

Research developed with communities and partners can help identify practical approaches that support health, wellbeing and sustainable livelihoods.
