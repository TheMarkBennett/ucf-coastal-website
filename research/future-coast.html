Research theme 06

Future Coast

Today’s science, tomorrow’s coastline. Prepare coastal leaders and connect research with society through education, communication and shared solutions.

Leadership & communication

Equip people to shape what comes next.

The future of coastal places depends on people who can understand, manage and protect them.

The Future Coast theme connects interdisciplinary education, hands-on research and real-world experience. It emphasizes skills that help emerging leaders work across science, engineering, policy and society.

Science communication and storytelling can make complex coastal research more accessible and useful to communities, policymakers and decision-makers.
