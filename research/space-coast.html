Space Coast

Coasts to cosmos. Explore the connections between space innovation and the stewardship of Florida’s coastal environments and communities.

Florida’s space-adjacent coast

Connect space innovation with coastal stewardship.

Florida’s Space Coast brings together communities, infrastructure, ecosystems and space activity in a distinctive coastal setting.

The proposed Space Coast theme includes research on coastal resilience near launch facilities and surrounding communities, environmental effects associated with development and activity, and the use of satellites and remote sensing to observe coasts.

Faculty content also identifies cultural heritage research as part of this theme. Current projects and methods should be connected to the relevant research teams and authoritative sources.
