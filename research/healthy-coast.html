Healthy Coast

Flourishing nature at the heart of the coastline. Understand, protect and restore the ecosystems that sustain coastal life.

Ecosystems & restoration

Healthy ecosystems support resilient coasts.

Coastal habitats support biodiversity, improve water quality and provide benefits to people and wildlife.

UCF Coastal’s proposed research theme explores how environmental change and human activity affect estuaries, wetlands and nearshore habitats. Areas described in faculty content include oyster reefs, seagrass beds, mangroves, living shorelines and nature-based approaches to coastal resilience.

Connecting ecology, engineering, policy and community engagement can help build knowledge for ecosystem protection and restoration.
