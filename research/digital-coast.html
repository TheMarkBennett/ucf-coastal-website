Research theme 04

Digital Coast

Smarter decisions through data, models, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

Data & technology

Connect coastal observations with decisions.

Digital tools can help researchers and decision-makers observe change, test scenarios and understand complex coastal systems.

The proposed Digital Coast theme brings together artificial intelligence, machine learning, advanced modeling, sensors, drones, satellites and remote sensing. These approaches can support analysis of flooding, shoreline change and other coastal conditions.

Digital twins and connected data systems may help users explore potential options before implementing them in real places.

Related research

Digital methods connect with hazard assessment, infrastructure, ecosystem monitoring and community planning.
