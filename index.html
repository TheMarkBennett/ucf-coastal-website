Why coasts matter

Coasts connect people, nature and the systems we depend on.

Coastlines support communities, ecosystems, food systems, tourism, transportation and critical infrastructure. UCF Coastal brings together science, engineering, policy and community expertise to understand coastal change and explore evidence-based responses.
