Center for Integrated Coastal Research

Our coast: where people, nature and the ocean meet

Coasts sustain communities, ecosystems and economies. UCF Coastal brings people and disciplines together to understand coastal change and shape better ways forward.

Our coast is changing

One shoreline. Many connected futures.

Coasts support communities, ecosystems, food systems, tourism, transportation and critical infrastructure. Their health affects people far beyond the water’s edge.

Sea-level rise, storms, flooding, erosion, marine heat waves, pollution, ecosystem loss and rapid development are increasing pressure on coastal places. Understanding how these forces interact is essential to managing change and sustaining coastal communities and natural systems.

Florida is a defining part of this story: its coastline shapes the state’s communities, economy, environment and identity.

More than a shoreline

Coastal systems connect people, nature and the ocean. Research helps make those connections visible and informs the choices that shape their future.

Champion for the coast

Connecting expertise to complex coastal questions

UCF’s Center for Integrated Coastal Research brings together researchers from across the University of Central Florida to address challenges no single discipline can solve alone.

We connect science, engineering, technology, policy and communities to build knowledge and develop practical approaches to coastal change. By working across traditional academic boundaries, researchers can examine coastal challenges from multiple perspectives and work toward solutions for people and the planet.

Our work spans Florida and coastlines around the world, with a shared aim: healthier, more resilient and prosperous coasts.

Our vision

Help coasts thrive in the face of change.

By better understanding the world’s coastlines, we help communities, ecosystems and economies thrive in the face of change.

Our mission

01 · Advance coastal knowledge

Understand coastal systems

Conduct interdisciplinary research on how natural processes and human activities shape the health, resilience and future of coasts.

02 · Turn science into action

Put knowledge to work

Partner with communities, agencies and collaborators to share clear, timely information that can support management and decision-making.

03 · Develop future leaders

Learn and communicate

Educate and mentor future coastal scientists and practitioners, helping them conduct research and communicate it effectively.
