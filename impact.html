Impact in many forms

Built through listening, collaboration and communication

Coastal challenges are complex and connected. Addressing them means working alongside people who live on, manage and make decisions about our coasts.

UCF Coastal aims to build impact into research from the beginning: identifying important questions with partners, co-developing solutions and communicating findings so they are clear, timely and useful.

Impact in action

Research connected to decisions and communities

01

Public health · Florida

Bringing coastal risk into public health assessment

UCF Coastal researchers are working with the Florida Department of Health on its statewide Public Health Risk Assessment Tool, bringing information on hazards, social vulnerability and critical infrastructure into public health risk assessment.

Partner Florida Department of Health

02

Hazard planning · Central Florida

Developing tools for regional hazard planning

With the East Central Florida Regional Planning Council, UCF Coastal researchers are developing automated tools intended to support hazard planning, emergency management and resilience decisions.

Partner East Central Florida Regional Planning Council

03

Ecosystem restoration · Indian River Lagoon

Testing approaches to restore the lagoon

A $4.9 million partnership with the Florida Department of Environmental Protection and other agencies is testing an innovative approach to Indian River Lagoon restoration.

Related research includes living shorelines, oyster reefs and seagrass, new approaches to Florida red tide, and tools to improve marine protected-area management.

Partners Florida Department of Environmental Protection and other agencies

04

Coastal resilience · United States

Developing sea-level information for coastal installations

UCF Coastal researchers are developing improved sea-level information to help protect more than 1,800 coastal Department of Defense installations.

Focus Sea-level change and coastal risk

05

Delta communities · Mississippi River

Working across a changing delta

UCF Coastal researchers are working with communities and decision-makers through the $22 million Mississippi River Delta Transition Initiative.

Focus Research, communities and decisions across the Mississippi River Delta

06

Partnerships · Florida and beyond

Connecting research with coastal practice

Across Florida, the United States and internationally, UCF Coastal researchers work with agencies and communities on coastal hazards, ecosystem stewardship, resilience and environmental management.

Collaborators Government, industry, nonprofits and communities
