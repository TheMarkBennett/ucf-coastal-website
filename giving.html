Why give to coastal research

Help champion the coast

UCF Coastal is committed to building a healthier, more resilient and prosperous future for coasts and the communities that depend on them.

By bringing together expertise across science, engineering, policy and community engagement, the center advances research, educates future coastal leaders and works to translate knowledge into practical solutions.

Gifts of any size can support student opportunities, interdisciplinary research, fieldwork, community engagement, and the tools and resources needed to address challenges facing Florida’s coast and coastal regions around the world.

Thank you for helping UCF Coastal champion the coast.

Long-term giving

Planned gifts

Planned gifts can be considered as part of financial and estate planning. Options may include bequests, life insurance, retirement assets or life-income gifts. Contact UCF Foundation to discuss arrangements and next steps.

Permanent funds

Endowments
